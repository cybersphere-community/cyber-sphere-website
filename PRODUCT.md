# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
* **Students**: Aspiring cybersecurity enthusiasts looking for foundational knowledge and CTF training.
* **Working Professionals**: Security analysts, developers, and engineers looking for hands-on, non-corporate workshops.
* **CEOs & Leadership**: Executive stakeholders evaluating security posture, training programs, and community-sourced talent.

## Product Purpose
* To serve as the digital home for the **Cyber Sphere Community**—a platform dedicated to cybersecurity education, workshops, and open research.
* Empower individuals with real-world, practical security skills that bridge the gap between academic theory and industry practice.

## Positioning
* **No Corporate Agenda**: Guided strictly by researchers, defenders, and creators.
* **Open & Collaborative**: 100% open-source community sharing actual, reproducible security findings and materials.
* **Direct Handson Value**: Concrete workshops, CTF training, and job-readiness pathways.

## Operating Context
* Public web visitors exploring resources, upcoming meetups, and articles.
* Community members looking to participate as speakers or attendees in workshops.

## Capabilities and Constraints
* **Platform**: Responsive Web.
* **Color constraints**: Theme must remain white/light. Accent colors must utilize the logo's red, green, and black.
* **Aesthetic goal**: Clean, minimal, non-generic (avoiding standard "AI slop" floating shapes and blurs). Touch of technical/cyber feel.

## Brand Commitments
* **Logo Palette**: Cyber Red, Cyber Green, and Black.
* **Voice**: Professional, technical, authoritative yet accessible.

## Evidence on Hand
* Detailed speaker profiles (Harsh, Naman, Brijesh, Himanshi, S Aravind).
* List of completed/upcoming workshops (Linux Exploitation, Cloud Security, IoT Hacking, etc.).

## Product Principles
1. **Structure Over Fluff**: Prioritize functional, high-readability layouts.
2. **Technical Credibility**: Use precise terminology and clear, real data over vague marketing assertions.
3. **Tactile Interaction**: Design micro-interactions that feel mechanical, quick, and responsive.
4. **Light & Precise**: Keep the canvas light and clean with surgical color placement.
